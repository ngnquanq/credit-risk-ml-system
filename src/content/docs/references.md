Design decisions in the ADRs cite these sources by name. The technical report keeps its own numbered list.

## Machine learning and fairness

- S. Barocas, M. Hardt and A. Narayanan. *Fairness and Machine Learning: Limitations and Opportunities*. MIT Press, 2023. [fairmlbook.org](https://fairmlbook.org)
- M. Hardt, E. Price and N. Srebro. Equality of opportunity in supervised learning. *NeurIPS*, 2016.
- S. M. Lundberg and S.-I. Lee. A unified approach to interpreting model predictions. *NeurIPS*, 2017.
- T. Chen and C. Guestrin. XGBoost: A scalable tree boosting system. *KDD*, 2016.
- D. Pedreshi, S. Ruggieri and F. Turini. Discrimination-aware data mining. *KDD*, 2008.
- Home Credit Group. *Home Credit Default Risk*. Kaggle, 2018.
